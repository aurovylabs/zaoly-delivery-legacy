The handover
comes first

A working handover starts with integration and ownership. AI earns its place when it makes varied customer language usable without erasing uncertainty.

One Source means each claim has an explicit authority. Every Touchpoint means the next person can continue the customer’s work. Five things must survive: the goal, completed work, relevant facts, permission and responsibility.

Historical and cross-industry perspectives test this proposal against existing ideas and different operating conditions. Published effects support bounded parts of the argument. The economics remains an illustrative scenario: seven recovery minutes and a 3:1 savings split are assumptions, not findings.
